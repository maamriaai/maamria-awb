# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), kept short.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances of any kind.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, the Maamria AI dashboard's community channels — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour may be sent privately to:

> **conduct@maamria.com**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use the following ladder when responding to violations:

1. **Correction** — private warning, clarification of why the behaviour was inappropriate, and a request for a public apology if relevant.
2. **Warning** — formal warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
